Size and placement

Lid top seen from above, front edge at the bottom. All dimensions in inches.

  • Lid top is the only clean panelIt is the largest flat face with no folds, flaps or seams. The front, back and ends all carry a fold line or a rolled edge.
  • 7 × 5 in, centeredLeaves 0.875 in to each side and 1.125 in to the back hinge and the front fold, so the label never crosses a crease and the lid opens freely.
  • Reads from the frontThe 7 in side runs parallel to the front edge, so the type reads the right way up when the box faces the customer.
  • Standard size7 × 5 in is a stock label size at most printers, which keeps die costs down.
  • Application toleranceAbout 1/8 in of hand-placement drift in any direction still keeps a safe gap from every fold.

To confirm before printing

01
Brand spelling

The brief writes "DekoTech". The official name and logo in the 2026 brand guideline are "Deckotech", so the label uses Deckotech.

02
Supplier specs

No supplier specs were received. Files follow standard label specs (above) and can be re-exported quickly if the printer asks for something different.

03
Orange on press

CMYK cannot hit PMS 172 C exactly. Ask for a printed proof, or print the orange as a Pantone spot if the supplier offers it.

04
Box measurement check

Placement is based on the 8.75 × 7.25 in lid. A quick test with a printed 7 × 5 in paper cut-out on a real box confirms the fit before the full run.
